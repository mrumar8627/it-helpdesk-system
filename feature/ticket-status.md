# Ticket Status

## Objective

To understand the different statuses used to track the progress of IT support tickets.

## What Is Ticket Status?

Ticket status shows the current stage of a support request.

It helps IT support staff understand which tickets are new, being investigated, waiting for information, resolved, or closed.

## Common Ticket Statuses

### New

The ticket has been created but has not yet been investigated.

Example:

A user reports that their computer is not connecting to Wi-Fi.

Status: New

### Open

The ticket has been reviewed and requires investigation or action.

Example:

The technician has received the ticket and is preparing to investigate the problem.

Status: Open

### In Progress

The technician is actively working on the problem.

Example:

The technician is checking the computer's network configuration.

Status: In Progress

### Pending

The ticket is temporarily waiting for information, access, equipment, or another action.

Example:

A required replacement device has been ordered.

Status: Pending

### Resolved

The technician has applied a solution and believes the reported problem has been fixed.

Example:

The network configuration was corrected and connectivity was restored.

Status: Resolved

### Closed

The issue has been resolved, verified, and the ticket is completed.

Status: Closed

## Basic Ticket Status Flow

New
↓
Open
↓
In Progress
↓
Pending
↓
In Progress
↓
Resolved
↓
Closed

Not every ticket needs to use every status.

For example, a simple problem may move directly from:

New → In Progress → Resolved → Closed

## Example

### Problem

A staff member reports that Microsoft Word is not opening.

### Ticket

Ticket ID: HD-007

Category: Software

Priority: Medium

Initial Status: New

### Investigation

The technician begins checking the application.

Status: In Progress

### Finding

The application requires an available update.

### Action

The required update is installed.

### Verification

Microsoft Word is opened successfully and tested.

Status: Resolved

### Final Status

After the issue is confirmed as resolved:

Closed

## Status Updates

A technician should update the ticket status when the condition of the ticket changes.

Example:

New
↓
In Progress
↓
Pending
↓
In Progress
↓
Resolved
↓
Closed

Each status change should represent an actual stage of the support process.

## Why Ticket Status Is Important

Ticket status helps support teams:

- Track active problems
- Identify unresolved tickets
- Monitor work in progress
- Identify tickets waiting for information
- Confirm resolved issues
- Maintain organized support records

## Good Practices

When changing a ticket status:

- Select the correct status.
- Add relevant notes.
- Record important actions.
- Do not mark a ticket as resolved without testing the solution.
- Close the ticket only after the issue has been completed according to the support process.
